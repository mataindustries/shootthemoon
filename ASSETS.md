# Shoot the Moon asset provenance

Status: First Strike release-candidate runtime asset record, 2026-08-30

First Strike ships two external lunar textures. Both are unmodified downloads
from NASA's Scientific Visualization Studio (SVS) CGI Moon Kit. All other
runtime visuals are generated by this repository and do not contain third-party
art. The release-candidate pass introduced no additional external asset or
license dependency.

## NASA lunar textures

subject to NASA's media-usage rules, including no implied NASA endorsement and
separate restrictions on NASA identifiers. See [SVS help and permissions](https://svs.gsfc.nasa.gov/help/)

## Project-authored runtime visuals

- The invasion capsule is assembled from indexed Three.js primitives at
  runtime, including its animated hatch and illuminated bay. It has no external
  model or texture.
- The close-range curved terrain overlay and its 128 × 128 single-channel bump
  texture are deterministic procedural data seeded from the selected canonical
  coordinate.
- Rocks and distant ridge stones are deterministic instanced geometry. Their
  placement preserves the three bounded miner routes.
- The compact wheeled miner, animated drill, visible cargo, Lunar Ore deposits,
  scanner indicators, staged extractor, and orbital outpost signal are
  code-authored true-3D Three.js geometry with shared or instanced resources.
- The release-candidate capsule, miner, extractor, integrated launch platform,
  lunar warhead, exhaust, and mechanical launch details are code-authored from
  Three.js primitives and repository materials. No GLB, texture atlas, stock
  model, or third-party kit is used.
- Commander Vesper's Null Meridian architecture, broken-crown command
  silhouette, sensor mast, scan presentation, damaged state, and wreckage are
  code-authored true-3D geometry. They do not reuse a recolored player model.
- The strike flash, radial regolith sheet, ballistic ejecta, dark fragments,
  impact illumination, crater floor, irregular rim, ejecta field, thermal
  points, and close/orbital scar presentations are deterministic code-authored
  geometry, points, lights, and materials. No explosion footage, sprite sheet,
  decal image, or cutscene video is present.
- The starfield, impact dust, surface dust, mining sparks, and fragments are
  deterministic point or primitive geometries. They use no bitmap sprites.
- The landing marker is true 3D torus, ring, and beam geometry.
- The orbital claim beacons — the camera-facing bracket rings, leader lines, and
  faction glyphs that keep the player outpost and Null Meridian readable at
  orbital distance — are a single merged code-authored buffer geometry per
  marker. They use no sprite, bitmap, or font atlas.
- `public/favicon.svg` is a hand-authored vector drawn from the repository
  palette (space, lunar, and player amber). `public/favicon.png` is a 32 x 32
  raster of that same file, provided for browsers without SVG icon support.
  Neither contains third-party art.

## Project-authored audio and haptics

The optional sound layer is synthesized at runtime with the browser Web Audio
API. Oscillators, filters, gain envelopes, and a deterministic generated noise
buffer produce interface confirmations, capsule and miner machinery, drilling,
rival and scan textures, arming, ignition, flight, impact, and completion cues.
The synthesized effects use no audio files; the only audio files are the
adaptive music above.

Optional ignition and impact haptics use the feature-detected browser Vibration
API. Vibration patterns are code, not third-party assets, and the experience
does not require them.

No external capsule, robot, deposit, extractor, rival structure, warhead,
particle, explosion, sound, icon, billboard, UI-image, or video asset is present
in this milestone.
